For: Content editors, Site admins and Super admins; assigned page editors within their page access. Last checked: 2026-11-03.

Choose the hero on the page. Hero slides shows each photo, title and On switch.
Drag to reorder, or open a slide’s actions for Move up, Move down and Remove.
The buttons are the same keyboard path as dragging. Open an existing slide to
edit its title and introduction first. Photo and crop keeps picture choices,
description, focus and the optional phone image together; it opens when you click
the photo on the canvas, or when a new slide needs a picture. How the slider
works keeps the behavior explanation available without lengthening the list. The separate twelve-slide
ceiling is removed. The hero shares the CMS’s existing bounded-repeat guard
(48 items) and content-size safeguards; no new limit is imposed for normal hero
use. Editors do not choose a separate hover or carousel design.

Add slide offers stories already saved in this site, with search at the top.
Choosing one copies its title, description, photo and Read More destination into
a new slide. Later story edits do not silently replace those chosen words.
Roles without Stories access go directly to an empty slide. This picker reads
the CMS only; it never checks or refreshes the SAWSO public stories feed.

choose or upload a picture, add its description and set its focus point. Back or Cancel removes a new addition and all its fields. Done keeps
it. A title, photo and photo description (or an explicit decorative choice) are
needed before Done. The normal publishing checks also catch an unfinished
slide, including when publishing from another screen.

The shared three-slide upgrade keeps every saved word, photo, focus point and
button destination. It replaces numbered fields with list items as a reviewed
draft. It does not publish that draft automatically.

On controls whether a slide appears publicly. Show only between dates uses the
time zone displayed beside the date controls. The start begins at midnight;
the end date is included. Other available slides continue afterward. Dates are
checked when the page loads; a page already open needs reloading to pick up a
changed date window. Off slides stay available for editing.

Public slides change every six seconds. Hover and keyboard focus pause them;
reduced motion turns off automatic changes and smooth motion. One round Next
slide button uses the icon set’s open chevron, beside the dots in their pill at
the bottom right. Next slide and the labelled dots work with a keyboard. Without JavaScript the
first available slide shows and ordinary anchors reach every other available
slide. Several heroes have distinct anchors, so one cannot jump into another.
The editor is stationary from its first render. It keeps the slide containing
the selected field in view while typing. Choosing a canvas dot or chevron also
opens that slide’s fields. The left list has one row per slide rather than all
numbered fields, and still searches each slide’s full words. A new slide panel
starts fresh when switching to another hero section.

Save draft, review and publish through the page’s existing publishing flow.
History retains the earlier page and numbered-slide version. The wider editor
toolbar, role layout and leave-guard cleanup remain separate Round 3 work; this
new list alone does not complete EditorClean or the full admin comparison.
