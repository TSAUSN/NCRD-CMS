# Titles and links

Edit a title in place, and change link destinations without losing your place.

For: Content editors, Site admins, Super admins and assigned page editors within their access. Last checked: 2026-10-31.

Click a section title on a native page to open Section title. Its outline stays
on the page. Type in Title to preview the words immediately. Back returns to
the section's full content; Done returns to This page. The existing panel divider
widens the panel and remembers its width. A new section starts in its complete
content view, ready for its other fields too.

Link beside the title shows its words and destination. Choose Change to edit
it. Other links use the same Change control in their content field. Choose Page
on this site, Website, Email or Phone. Only published pages appear in the page
chooser; a manual path or #link within a page remains available under Website.
Email and Phone add the appropriate link prefix for you.

Open in a new tab and nofollow are inside Change. Your choices preview as you
type and stay in the draft. Done keeps them. Cancel, close or Escape restores
the field as it was when you opened Change, including its sample status. Other
page edits stay in place. Nothing publishes from this dialog. Site admins and
Super admins can reach existing button appearance controls under Advanced;
Editors only see link content and behavior.

Brought-in sites keep their existing link editing path. The wider EditorClean
layout, role pass and three-choice leave guard are separate work. The final
numbered acceptance walk remains required.

heading link stays beside the title without extending outside the page.
