# Coming up

Place reviewed, undated stories on a calendar and review their publication time.

For: Editors, Site admins and Super admins with publishing permission. Last checked: 2026-10-31.

Open Stories, then Coming up. The month shows individually scheduled stories,
including weekends. Previous and Next move between months. Times use your device's
time zone, named above the grid. A failed timer says Needs attention and links to
Publish. Existing group releases stay in Publish; this calendar does not guess
which current stories an older whole-list review contained.

Ready, no date yet contains unpublished, reviewed stories with a description,
photo and full text, and no story date. Hidden stories and stories removed from
the source stay out. Search this list by title. Add missing words/photo in the
story editor, and mark Reviewed in Stories when it is ready.

Drag a ready story onto a day, or choose its story row (a button named Choose day for the story) and enter a day.
Cancel or Escape closes this choice without saving anything. Review schedule
saves that story day as an ordinary draft, then opens the normal publishing
review. It proposes 9:00 AM in your device time zone; change the date/time there
if needed. Inspect View changes and the page checks, then confirm Schedule
selected changes. Nothing goes live just by dropping a story or saving its day.

A private site first needs its complete first-site publication review. If someone
changed Stories while you were choosing, reload before trying again; their words
are not overwritten. A failed review leaves the saved draft available. A retry
of the same already-saved day does not create another version.

New individual reviews keep the chosen Story and shared article fixed while
other stories are edited or published. Changing that story, its article, photo
or required site settings marks the calendar event Needs review. Open the story
to inspect it; use Publish to cancel its old timer before scheduling a new review.
Older whole-list/imported/custom-article reviews retain the broader site checks.
Normal Story saves preserve unchanged versions and public/private state.
