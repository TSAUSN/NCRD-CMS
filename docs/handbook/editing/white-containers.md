# White containers on national pages

The national template keeps content on a white surface against the grey page.

For: Editors, Site admins and Super admins. Last checked: 2026-10-30.

National library sections arrange their own title and white content card. Other
sections receive a white container from the template automatically, including
reports, accountability and team lists. Editors do not need to add a background.
The editing canvas and public page use the same rule. Other site designs and
standalone section thumbnails retain their own presentation.

The surface rule has passed focused renderer checks, type/style checks, both
app builds, affected Chromium journeys and two focused WebKit checks. Retained
Annual Reports, Financial Accountability and Our Team were captured at
1440/1024/390. The wider template rollout and full Round 3 acceptance remain
in progress. Typography still uses the listed stand-ins until the kit step. No hosted deployment is
