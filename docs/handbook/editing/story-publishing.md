# Publish or schedule a story

Save the story, review its exact saved changes, then publish or choose a time.

For: Editors and administrators with publishing rights. Last checked: 2026-11-04.

## Before you start

- **Save draft** keeps your words private. A draft does not need a Story date.
- Publishing and scheduling need a Story date and a ready, described photo.
- An individual review covers this Story and its changed article. Other Stories’
  drafts stay private. A private site needs **Review first site publication**.
- Content creators use **Submit for review** instead of publishing themselves.

## Steps

1. Open the Story, finish its words, tags, date and photo, then choose **Save draft**.
2. Choose **Preview** to check the saved Story with the site's draft appearance.
   Choose **Done** to return to writing.
3. Choose **Publish**. Changed words save before the review opens.
4. Check the named changes. Choose **View changes** to compare **Live now** with
   **Saved changes**, then **Done** to return to the review.
5. Resolve the listed checks. Check that the selected Story and article are the
   changes you intend to publish, then choose **Publish selected** (with the
   selected count) to open the final confirmation.
6. Choose **Publish selected** in the confirmation. Keep the window open until the completion message appears;
   updating feeds can take a minute.
7. Open the public article and check its words, photo and feed placement. If the
   result is uncertain, check **History** before retrying.

## Schedule instead

1. In the Story editor, choose **Schedule** to save and review the same exact targets.
2. In **Publish later**, choose the date and time in the displayed time zone.
3. Review the changes and findings, then confirm **Schedule selected changes**.
4. Check the request in **Coming up**. **Needs review** means its saved targets changed.
5. To replace an old timer, use **Publish** to cancel it before scheduling a fresh review.

The Story date is the day shown with the Story. Scheduling controls when the
reviewed version becomes public. A future Story date also keeps it out of feeds
until that day.

## Submit for review

1. As a Content creator, choose **Submit for review**. The Story saves privately first.
2. Read the saved-change summary and add an optional note.
3. Choose **Submit for review** in the dialog. **Cancel** returns to the saved draft.
4. Open **Reviews** to read comments or returned feedback. Changed submitted words,
   photo, tags or settings need a new request; editing another Story does not.

Reviewers use [Review saved changes](../publishing/review-changes.md).

## Limits

- Editing this Story, its article, photo or required settings requires a fresh review.
  Other Stories can still progress during an individual Story review or schedule.
- Whole-list reviews, imported/custom articles and older prepared reviews keep their
  wider site checks. Prepare a fresh individual review for individual targets.
- A duplicate Newsroom position or sixth live feature is refused. Publish a changed
  featured group together from the site's **Publish** screen.
- Story/feed end dates and automatic change-back dates are not supported.
- Choosing **Back** with unsaved work offers **Keep editing**, **Discard changes**
  and **Save draft and leave**. A failed save keeps your words and the dialog open.
