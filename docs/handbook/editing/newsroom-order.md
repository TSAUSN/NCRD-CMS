# Newsroom order

Keep up to five stories first in the Newsroom while sector pages keep date order.

For: People who can edit Stories. Last checked: 2026-11-03.

In Stories, click Feature under a story’s tags and review controls. The star
saves a private draft immediately. Featured removes it from that group. Keep
using the same tag chips, Reviewed, Hide from feeds, search and filters.

Newsroom order shows Featured and Everything else beside What visitors see.
Drag featured stories into order, or use their Move up/Move down buttons. Drag
an ordinary story into Featured, or click its star. Everything else stays newest
first by story date; there is no way to reorder that whole list. Five featured
places are available. Remove one before adding a sixth.

Hidden, removed, future and expired stories stay out of visitor feeds. A featured
story that is not currently eligible says so; its reserved place remains until
you remove its star. By Project Sector, By Map and sector pages keep tag/date
order. Recently Published and unfiltered news lists put featured stories first.

Saving shows progress. If a save cannot be confirmed, your proposed order stays
visible. Retry, or Use saved order to discard the unsaved proposal. Leaving with
an unsaved proposal offers the shared keep/save/discard choices. A saved order
still waits for the normal Publish review before visitors see it. Successful saves
show a short acknowledgement; the draft banner explains whether publication
is still needed.

Existing sites may first show Update Stories. A Site admin adds the optional
controls through that button as a draft. This preserves article words and
photos. New sites already have them. The numbered final acceptance walk is
still pending; development verification is recorded separately.

A single-story publication or schedule is refused if it would leave duplicate
featured positions or more than five live features. The review explains how
to publish the changed Newsroom group together from the site Publish screen.
Independent story words can still publish separately. Nothing goes live on
a refused review.

Local verification: both new Chromium and WebKit ordering/Author journeys pass.
The WebKit regression verifies the exact full new-story publish/schedule journey
and both repeated-hour forms. The final acceptance walk remains open.

Updating these optional controls leaves unchanged stories alone. Saving one
author or star creates only the needed draft. A compatible tag-policy update
also preserves untouched story versions. A different field change can still
require the normal explicit content upgrade.
