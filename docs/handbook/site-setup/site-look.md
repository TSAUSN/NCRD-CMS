# Site look drafts

Preview site-wide appearance choices privately, then review them for publishing.

For: People with site-look access. Last checked: 2026-11-02.

Open Site look. The template decides which colours, type and button choices are
available. Template ownership and contrast checks remain enforced. Fonts
have their own tab and approved kit connection.

Starts from contains the existing presets. Four colour rows keep Brand, Accent,
Dark and Light; Advanced colours shows the full role table. Fonts and all visual
button choices remain in the controls. Logo and shared parts links to Header &
menu and Footer. These compact cards scroll together beside the real-page preview. The preview
page selector and Desktop/Tablet/Phone choices share one toolbar at both admin
widths. The panel can still be widened and remembers its width. It reserves preview
space at 1024; double-click its divider to restore the starting width.

Each colour row keeps its picker and full hex value; contrast and Reset sit below.
Advanced colours reveals the remaining roles. More font choices and More button
options use the same disclosure control as the rest of Admin. An Approved pair
chip identifies the explicit national red/white exception; it does not claim AA.

Save draft, Revert to live and Publish site look stay together at the bottom.
Save draft keeps your choices private. Publish opens the saved-change review;
it does not skip checks. The ordinary revert choice also keeps its confirmation.

When choosing another CMS screen, Keep editing returns to unsaved choices. Save
draft and leave validates and saves privately before opening your destination.
Discard changes restores local saved choices; it does not revert the public site.
An invalid choice or failed save keeps the fields here. For a conflicting saved
design, check the saved state before trying again.

This leave behavior was checked locally. The screen comparison records actual controls/preview differences; reviewer visual
review and final acceptance remain open. Browser history has separate recovery
limits; no Google hosting or live site was used.
