# Social accounts

Keep the site's account addresses in one place, then publish their shared use.

For: Admins and Super admins. Last checked: 2026-11-01.

Open Settings → Social accounts. The rows are Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn
and YouTube. A previously configured TikTok account remains available. Existing
Footer accounts are offered as the starting choices; opening the tab changes
nothing. Empty rows do not invent public account addresses.

Enter the full https address. Facebook, Instagram and X also have an optional
handle. Switch In footer on for an account that should appear there. Clear the
handle and switch In footer off before removing an account address.

Save draft keeps the settings private. Schedule or Publish saves your changes
and opens the normal release review. Confirm the review before anything changes
on the public site. The first release of a private site still needs its complete
site review. Leaving unsaved fields offers Keep editing, Save draft and leave,
or Discard changes. A conflicting save keeps your fields and offers a separate
view of saved settings; compare those before reloading.

The Footer reads accounts from this tab. A new or explicitly updated On-page
navigation section can choose Links from → Site social accounts. That section
uses all configured account addresses, independently of In footer. Its account
links are managed in Settings. Its heading remains page content. Switching back
to This section restores its own saved links. Existing chapter-owned links and
older saved layouts keep their original source until explicitly changed.

An empty connected social section is hidden from visitors. Editors see its
heading and a note pointing to Settings. There is no embed code or analytics
setting. Shared links work without JavaScript.
