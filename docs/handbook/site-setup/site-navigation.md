# Finding site tools

Choose the site before editing its content or shared settings.

For: Editors, Admins and Super admins. Last checked: 2026-11-01.

The current site is above Search or jump to. Open the site chooser to switch to
another site you can access, or All sites. Escape closes it and returns focus.
Loading or an error does not pretend there are no sites; All sites remains an
available way out. Unsaved-work choices still apply when switching sites.

Native sites group Header & menu, Footer and Site look under Site-wide, then
Publish and Settings under Publishing. Content roles do not see site-wide
administration. An administrator on another site does not gain those controls
here. Advanced groups section and template management for existing authorized
administrators. Workspace retains global Home, All sites, Inbox, People & access,
Domains and Activity only where existing access permits. Original-design sites
keep their existing labels and commands. Database permissions remain enforced.

The sidebar can still fold, and remembers the choice. Below the editing width,
Open navigation opens a dismissible panel. These controls do not publish work.
