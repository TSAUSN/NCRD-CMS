# Review publishing checks

Summary: Fix the listed problems or record the allowed reason before confirming publication.
Who it is for: Editors, Site admins and Super admins who can publish.
Last checked: 2026-10-31.

Publish prepares a review before anything goes live. A repeated link to the same
unpublished address appears once in the checks; each different address remains
listed. Publishing the destination resolves every repeated link to that address. Repeating the
same problem does not remove its blocking status.

Sample content needs its own confirmation. Other permitted exceptions need a
written reason. Permissions and source-code approval cannot be bypassed by a
reason. A failed preview leaves the public site unchanged.

The release review retains the checked findings. The full Round 3 publish layout,
plain-word saved changes, leave guard and scheduling walk remain in progress.
