# Review saved changes

Read the exact saved differences before publishing or restoring a draft.

For: People with publishing access. Last checked: 2026-11-04.

## Before you start

- Opening a review does not publish. The review uses the saved version it names.
- A later edit or conflict needs a fresh review; it does not replace the version
  already being compared. **Cancel** keeps the saved draft.
- Menu, header, footer and shared-list changes have their own publication and permissions.

## Steps

1. In the page editor, choose **Publish**. The editor saves current changes and
   waits for queued saves before showing the review.
2. Read the named differences. **Show** returns to the changed section for editing;
   removed sections and page details have no **Show** action.
3. Choose **Cancel** or press Escape to keep working privately. Choose
   **Schedule instead** for the date review, or **Publish** to continue through
   the normal checks and confirmation.
4. To review several saved changes, open the site's **Publish** screen and its
   **Ready** tab. Tick only the intended rows and choose **View changes** on each.
5. Choose **Publish selected** to prepare the selected review. Resolve the checks,
   confirm the exact selected changes, then check the public result.
6. If a saved comparison is stale, choose **Refresh Publish**, read the current
   differences and prepare a fresh review. Your local writing remains available
   through the editor's recovery controls.

## Restore as new draft

1. Open the page's **History**, either in the toolbar or under **More page actions**.
2. Choose an earlier version in the left queue. Read its changes and read-only preview.
   **Compare with another version** lets you choose another saved comparison.
3. Choose **Restore as new draft**.
4. In the confirmation, read that the live page will not change, then choose
   **Create draft**. The editor opens the new private draft.
5. Choose **Preview**, then **Open preview**, to check the restored draft.
6. Review and publish it through the normal page flow when ready. Restoring alone
   leaves the public page and all earlier versions in place.

**Show only changes** hides the preview; **Show page previews** brings it back.
**Clear filters**, **Older versions**, **Newer versions** and **Show automatic saves**
help find a version. **Whole site** opens release history when your access allows it.

## Review a submitted Story or list

1. Open **Reviews** and choose the request in the left queue. Use **Lists** for list requests.
2. Read **Changes** and **Checks**. **Preview** shows the submitted version;
   **All saved values** opens both full list snapshots when reviewing a list.
3. Open **Discussion** to read or write feedback. Choose **Add comment** to send it.
4. For a Story, choose **Approve and publish** to publish that submitted Story and
   its article, or **Return with feedback** to leave the public site unchanged.
5. If the submitted Story or required settings changed, request another submission.
   Work on another Story does not replace this request's submitted version.

## Limits

- History previews block forms, scripts and top navigation. Use the editor's isolated
  **Preview** for imported layouts that need their original relative assets or scripts.
- Archived sites retain history and comparisons but offer no restore or publication action.
- Shared numbers and other shared content have their own history.
- **Save draft and leave** keeps an unsent review comment only in this browser and
  account. **Add comment** sends it. A failed private save keeps your words visible.
- A stale or unavailable request cannot publish a newer version or open another request
  in its place. Signed-link comment-only reviews keep their separate access flow.
