# Save, review and publish a page

Keep writing private until the exact saved change is ready for visitors.

For: Editors and site administrators. Last checked: 2026-11-04.

## Before you start

- Saving never changes the public site. Only publishing does.
- Editors publish page content. A site's menu, header and footer are managed
  separately by a Site admin, so a page edit never changes the shared frame.
- Contributors and chapter volunteers cannot publish. They submit their change
  for review, and an authorised person approves it.

## Steps

1. Edit the page, then choose **Save draft**. Your work is saved privately.
2. Choose **Preview**, then **Open preview**, to see the saved page as visitors will.
3. Open **Publish** from the site menu. The **Ready** tab lists every saved change
   that is not live yet.
4. Tick only the changes you mean to publish. Shared settings, the menu and
   related content are listed as separate items. Do not publish other people's
   unrelated changes just to clear the list.
5. Choose **View changes** on a row to see exactly what will change.
6. Choose **Publish selected**. Fix anything the checks list, or record the
   allowed reason (see [Checks](checks.md)), then confirm.
7. Open the public page and check it.

To publish at a later time instead, choose **Publish later…** in step 6. See
[Schedule a page](schedule-a-page.md).

## Going back to an older version

Open the page's **History** and choose **Restore as new draft**. This creates a
private draft from the older version; the public page does not change until you
review and publish that draft as above. See [Review changes](review-changes.md).

## Limits

- A newer draft needs its own review. Publishing reviews the exact saved version
  you checked, not later edits.
- The 2026 Annual Report needs the content editor’s review before it is published.
