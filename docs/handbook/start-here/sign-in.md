# Sign in

Use your invitation address to reach the sites assigned to you.

For: Invited editors and administrators. Last checked: 2026-11-02.

Enter your email on Sign in. The pending button prevents another submission.
The receipt offers Use a different email, which also lets you request a fresh
link by entering the same address again. An expired or already-used link shows
That link has expired; Request a new link returns to email entry. Signing in
never grants a site assignment by itself. Ask your site administrator for access.

In this local demo, sample staff use Continue to local CMS. Scoped chapter links
are recorded in the local outbox. Check the local outbox explicitly says No
email was sent; the demo operator opens the recorded link. About local sign-in
explains the two paths. Real email delivery was not exercised in the verification process.

Microsoft is the selected long-term sign-in direction; implementation remains planned. Before implementation, IT must provide its
approved tenant, application registration and responsible service administrator, approved
redirect addresses, credential handling, permissions/consent and group-to-CMS-role
mapping. Existing site/page scopes must remain explicit. Provider changes require approval before creating
an application registration, credentials, cloud configuration or a Microsoft sign-in control.
