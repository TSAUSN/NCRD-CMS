# Your content tasks

Home starts with the work your account can do.

For: Site editors, Content creators and assigned-page editors. Last checked: 2026-11-02.

Choose the current site above search. Home offers page editing, Stories and
Coming up where your current permissions allow them. Follow the site's name
to Overview, or choose All sites in the site chooser. Use the page and Story
actions available for your current assignment.

Needs attention uses saved missing tags, current public story gaps, the existing
schedule engine, visible drafts and review requests. Opening Home never refreshes
the public feed. Unknown story data shows an error with an Open Stories action
instead of invented counts. Empty attention shows the next step plainly.

Content menus hide site-wide controls. Lists appears only when there is a
non-story list to work on. Contributors do not receive a Publish menu item.
Assigned-page and chapter users retain only their existing scoped work. Saved
work reaches the public site through the normal review and publication path.
Original-design-only content Home keeps its existing task view.

Native Site overview shows saved page/list changes, open reviews and published
samples. It groups attention by kind; the complete page tree stays below the
work cards. Each tree row has one More actions menu containing Edit, View live
when published, and the management commands your role permits. Changes not live
uses the same status chip; hover for its complete saved state. Story articles
stay in Stories. Recent changes open the saved page,
and Site activity opens its authorized activity record. No analytics or public
source refresh runs when this screen opens.

Edit home page opens the real editor. Add a page uses the same private page
flow as Pages. Admins have Change site look; content-only accounts do not.
Super admins see the authorized site team and Manage people. Other roles see
only their own access. Chapter users retain their assigned page and approval
path. Imported sites keep their existing Overview.

Admin Home starts with Needs you, then Your sites and Jump back in. Attention uses
saved review requests, unpublished changes, published samples and recorded address
setup states. Site cards show a sandboxed, read-only saved home-page preview;
this preview is not a claim that the draft is live. Cards without a home page
say so. Open a card or its title to reach the site workboard. The card’s Actions
menu keeps Open and View site, plus saved page/publication counts. View site uses
the available address in a new tab; it does not publish a draft. Waiting badges
count saved changes, with the full status available on hover.

More tasks keeps the page and list shortcuts available below recent work.
Super admins also have New site. Test tools (this computer only) starts closed
and contains the existing Background jobs evidence.
Site admins see only authorized sites and no worker diagnostics. Archived sites
stay in their disclosure with content/history retained. Opening Home makes no
feed request, publication, hosting change or content save.

An **Original design · Open site** tile identifies an imported site whose
private thumbnail omits its approved site files. Open the site for its full
saved layout; this does not change its pages or publishing state.
