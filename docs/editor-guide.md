# Editor guide

Start at **Home** and choose **Edit … home page**, or open **All sites**, choose
your site and open a page. The editor shows the real page. **All sites** takes
you back in one click; **Back** returns to the CMS screen you came from.

## Find something quickly

Choose **Search or jump to…** in the sidebar, or press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on
Windows/Linux), including while editing inside the page. Search page titles,
list items or actions such as Settings. Only content you can access appears.
Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click a result. List items open their edit
form. Escape closes search; unsaved work still gets the usual warning before
navigation.

## Change words or a picture

Click the words or picture on the page. The blue outline and name show what you
selected. Click the words again to type, or use **Content** in the right panel.
For a picture, choose an existing image or upload one, then select **Use this image**.
Describe what the picture communicates, or mark it decorative when appropriate.

Use **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Phone** to check the result. The small zoom
control at the bottom changes the working view. **Fit** fits the page into the
available space. **Focus on page** gives the page more room.

Watch the save message. **All changes saved** means the CMS acknowledged the
save. If saving fails, keep the editor open and use the offered retry or recovery
copy. A conflict keeps your local work so you can compare it with the newer save.

Saving keeps work private. **Preview → Open preview** opens the visitor view of your draft.
Visitors see the previous published content until you publish.

## Keep the right part of a picture

Where **Focus point** appears, drag the blue dot onto the important part.
Card, Phone and Wide show common crop shapes as you move. **Fine-tune position**
has Across/Down controls; arrow keys move the dot, Shift makes a bigger move,
and Home centres it. **Use automatic crop** resets the choice.

Page pictures keep their own focus. Saving it keeps a private draft; publish
to update visitors. One Undo reverses one drag. A sample picture stays marked
Sample after cropping. Check the page’s Phone preview for its final layout.
The media library keeps a default for newly chosen pictures; changing it does
not move existing page pictures. Imported original-design pages keep their
reviewed picture styling.

## Add and arrange sections

Choose **+ Add section** at the desired gap, or select a section and choose
**Add section below**. The library names the destination. Use **Change** to
choose another position. **Preview** shows just that section at three sizes;
**Add** inserts it with finished example content.

Replace fields marked **Sample**. Publishing lists any examples that remain and
requires explicit confirmation before they go live.

The selected section's toolbar has Move up, Move down, Duplicate and Hide.
Its **More section actions** menu includes Change layout, Reset to live version and Delete.
Change layout offers layouts compatible with the selected content.

**Undo** and **Redo** follow page edits in order. Undo after Duplicate removes
the duplicate. A changed-field dot offers a revert for that field. Reset to live
version and Discard all unpublished changes ask for confirmation.

Hidden sections remain faded and dashed in the editor. The eye-off mark in
Structure helps you find them again. Use **Visibility** for phone, tablet,
desktop and Hide until ready. Check the visitor preview before publishing.

Drag panel edges for more room. Library and inspector widths are remembered
separately; double-click an edge to reset it. At narrower widths, use Structure,
Page and Edit to choose the area you need.

## Set up chapters and division teams

A site administrator opens **Settings → Chapter teams**, adds a division and
its web staff, then assigns that team in the chapter’s **Set up → People** step.
Leaving **National team only** gives division staff no access to that chapter.
Removing staff from a division immediately removes that division’s page access.

**Chapter defaults** sets how future chapters get updated; existing chapters
keep their own setting. Division staff start at **Home → Set up chapters** and
choose **+ Add a chapter**. The three steps cover its destination, sections and
people. Saving creates a private draft. Chapters with their own website need
no CMS page; choosing a chapter page later creates one with sample content.

In **Set up → Sections**, choose which parts appear and whether social links
lead the page. For a board, choose everyone or **First 8, then Show all**.
These choices stay private until the chapter page is published. A chapter set
to **Not listed yet** stays out of Find a chapter; a draft page also stays out
until its first publication. The point of contact is private.

## Invite a chapter editor in the local demo

Division staff open **Chapters**, set the chapter to **Chapter editors sign in;
the division approves**, then choose **Invite a volunteer**. Add their name and
email address. **Local outbox** records the invitation; no email is sent.

Choose **Copy sign-in link** and open it in a private browsing window. Each link
works once and expires after 20 minutes. The volunteer can request a fresh link
from **Sign in**; it appears in the same private outbox. A newer link replaces
earlier unused links. Only the chapter's division staff and site administrators
can read its recorded messages. This recording flow is available only locally.

## January and July chapter checks

In the local demo, a site administrator opens **Settings → Chapter checks**.
Choose the month and year, then **Record contact check**. **Open local outbox**
shows the messages. Chapters with editors are asked to check their page;
chapters maintained by the division are asked to send changes together, or
reply “all good”. A contact for several chapters receives one combined message.
Recording the same cycle again creates no duplicate messages.

The local worker records due January/July checks and new approval requests.
After five Monday–Friday working days in Eastern time, it records a reminder
for the division and the national backup. Nothing publishes automatically.
**Record waiting review messages** runs that check immediately. Turn checks or
reminders off using **Save check settings**. This demo never sends real email.

## Your chapter page

If you maintain one chapter, Home offers **Edit … Chapter** and shows only your
assigned work. Open **Board → Content** to move members up or down or choose
**Add a board member**. New members start with name, role and contact
placeholders. Use **Visibility → Hide until ready** to turn an optional section
off without deleting its contents.

**What is Echelon** and **Join us** show **Site editors only**. The site team
keeps these shared parts. New sections can be added at the end of the page;
controls that would move a protected part are unavailable.

Choose **Submit for review** when your changes are ready. Your division reviewer
opens the request in **Inbox**, checks the submitted page and chooses **Approve
and publish** or returns it with feedback. Approval publishes the reviewed
version, including its section choices, and updates Find a chapter. Saving alone
does not change the public directory.
Times in review and the local outbox are labelled in Eastern time.

## Speakers, sponsors and workshops

Click a person, logo or workshop on the page and choose its Edit action. Use
**Add speaker**, **Add sponsor** or **Add workshop** for a new item. The form
names the pages where the shared item appears. Saving creates a private list
draft; publishing the list updates those pages together.

For bulk work, open **Lists**. Choose a list, then use the table or cards.
Import and Export are in its menu. A site administrator manages list fields.
An empty list still offers its Add action.

BT2027 uses the original site's speaker cards, sponsor levels and workshop
table. Editing keeps those layouts. Its local source contains workshop content
dated 2023; confirm the content before using it for a real 2027 event.

## Menu and footer

On an ordinary CMS site, open **Site look** to work on its real pages. A site
administrator can change its colours, fonts, logo and buttons. Editors can
change shared header, menu and footer content.

BT2027 keeps its original reviewed design. Open **Menu & footer** for text and
links. Its shared header and footer may appear on different sets of pages;
the banner and preview choices show which pages the selected area affects.
Other original page menus remain editable on their own pages.

Rename menu items, use the arrows or drag to reorder, and **Indent** to put a
link inside a dropdown. **Move out** returns it to the top level. Add a page
using **Add page to menu**, or add a web link. Hiding/removing a menu item keeps
the page itself available. Check the real menu on Phone.

Save the site-wide draft, inspect another applicable preview page, then open
Publish to review the waiting changes. A saved footer change appears on all
its applicable draft previews before publication.

## Keep old links working

A site administrator opens **Settings → Redirects → Manage redirects**.
Choose **Add redirect**, enter the old address (for example, `/chapter/peoria`)
and choose its new page. A draft destination waits for publication. Once the
page is live, visitors using the old address go straight to its current address.
Editing a redirect takes effect immediately; removing one asks for confirmation.

For several addresses, choose **Import from a spreadsheet**. Save a CSV with
two columns, **Old address** and **New address**, using paths such as
`/chapter/northtexas` and `/chapters/north-texas`. Choose the file and **Preview
redirects**. Fix every error before importing. **Keep** means that row already
matches; **Update** replaces an existing mapping. No row is imported until the
whole preview is valid. If another person changes a mapping, preview again.

## Create a page

Open **Pages**, choose **Add page** and select a template. Event home, Speakers,
Program, Plan your visit, Sponsors / exhibitors, Resource page and Landing page
provide starting content. Give the page a clear name and address, replace its
examples, and add it to the menu when appropriate.

Open **Page settings** from the editor's More menu for the page checklist,
visitor title, description, sharing picture and address. Review its checks
before publishing.

## Review and publish

Open **Publish** to see **Changes ready to publish**, grouped by Pages, Lists
and shared site changes. **View changes** explains each item. Select the work
that should go live together, then **Publish selected** to review its effect.

Fix readiness findings using their links. Confirm any remaining sample content,
then confirm the release. **Publish later…** is available when monitored
scheduling is enabled. **In review** contains submitted work; **History** shows
what went live and who published it.

These status labels have the same meaning throughout the CMS:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | The page has never been published. |
| Live | Its saved page content matches the published version. |
| Live · changes not published | Saved page edits are waiting to go live. |
| In review | A reviewer is considering submitted work. |
| Scheduled | Publication has been booked for the shown time. |
| Hidden | The section or item is hidden from visitors. |
| Archived | Removed from the active site, with its history retained. |

Contributors save their permitted content and choose **Submit for review**.
Editors review and publish ordinary content. Site administrators also manage
site design and list fields. Super Admins manage site access and reviewed site
scripts/files. Only the controls available to your role can change content.

## Live testing

This guide describes the interface. A local demo sign-in and source comparison
do not grant access to a hosted or production site. Use only the site and role
you have been assigned. Keep real event content and permissions under the site
content editor’s review; publishing on a hosted address is a separate platform lead decision.

## Find work that needs attention

The site Overview has quick actions, its pages and Needs attention. Media offers
folders and a details drawer. Its Needs attention filter appears when there is
something to fix. Settings keeps general details, addresses, backup and the
separate Danger zone together.

For local setup, backups and hosting, see [Operations](operations.md).

## See who can edit

Super Admins use **People & access** to find someone by name, email, chapter or
division, or filter to a site. **Can edit** distinguishes whole sites, chapter
pages and divisions. **Edit access** shows those assignments separately.
Chapter access can be paused when the chapter switches to division-team editing.

For a chapter volunteer, use **Chapters → Invite a volunteer**. For division
staff, use **Settings → Division teams**. Saving account details preserves those
assignments. Deactivating an account pauses all its access. Whole-site access
covers every page, so review that separate control before granting it.

People without account-management access can open **Your access** to see their
own pages and responsibilities.
