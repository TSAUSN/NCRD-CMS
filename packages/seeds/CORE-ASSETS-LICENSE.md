# Built-in core library assets

They contain no third-party photographs, logos, fonts or source-site content. The maintainer may use, modify and distribute them without restriction (CC0-1.0 dedication). ## SAWSO theme assets

The maintainer supplied the exact Salvation Army shield for the SAWSO header and
footer. It is a brand asset, separate from the neutral CC0 sample set. The
labelled global-needs sample diagram is original vector artwork incorporating
that supplied shield; replace it with the approved reference infographic when
available. Approved photographs enter through Media and retain source/review
metadata. No fonts or photographs are hotlinked.
